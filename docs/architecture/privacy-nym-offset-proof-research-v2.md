## Required proof before a v2 design could pass review

1. Bind exact source/tokenizer/runtime artifacts and immutable original text/hash. Enumerate the exact added tokens, normalization, split, special-mask and decoder behavior; unsupported pathways fail closed. Do not assume replacement/prepending affects only one request prefix. Establish when each artificial marker is inserted after added-token splitting and how its normalized extent maps to the original. A hypothetical extra leading decoded space may be accounted for **only** by a proven insertion trace. No strip/lstrip, global offset subtraction, substring search or first-token deletion. An attended token combining artificial prefix and real text still contributes all its logits; a token representing only artificial bytes needs an explicit reviewed all-contributor policy, otherwise block.
2. Treat original literal U+2581 as an ambiguity hazard: Replace can collapse original space and literal marker to the same normalized symbol, and the decoder turns markers into spaces. Until a separately demonstrated lossless path exists, block original literal markers rather than invent their origin. Keep unknown/internal controls/literal-special cases blocked; no replacement-character recovery or normalization of source text.
3. Construct boundary tables over original Unicode scalars: cumulative UTF8 byte length and cumulative UTF16 unit length at every scalar endpoint. For each admitted scalar interval require its exact original substring and table-derived byte/UTF16 endpoints. Reject out-of-range/interior-byte/interior-surrogate positions, normalization mismatch and unaccounted characters. Combining marks and ZWJ components stay distinct scalars; no grapheme expansion. Verify both source-hash binding and endpoint conversion independently across the actual IPC boundary.
4. A prospective byte group must consist solely of consecutive recognized fallback IDs from the pinned inventory, not text merely resembling `<0xHH>`. Retain every token index in order; establish exact original scalar extent and exact strict UTF8 byte equality of the whole group. No replacement characters, arbitrary overlap union, nonconsecutive grouping, merged ordinary tokens or decode-and-search. Group boundaries must neither split a scalar nor consume neighbors; all overlapping contributors must be accounted for exactly once. Repeated offsets alone are insufficient evidence. Added tab/whitespace tokens require their own exact-content proof, not guessed missing-byte repair.
5. Grouping cannot bypass neural decisions. Every attended non-special contributor, including O, must retain finite81-label logits, unique argmax and confidence≥0.4. Preserve the minimum contributing confidence and all token provenance. Root must explicitly specify and review compatible group BIO/type transitions before implementation; disagreement, orphan I, kind changes, gaps or mixed O/entity interpretations block. No voting, averaging to overcome a failing token, dropped low-confidence pieces, repaired B/I or silent deduplication. A group reconstructing a character is not evidence of entity correctness.
6. Prove complete token accounting, ordered original coverage and no unassigned non-whitespace bytes. Prefix handling and grouping are narrow independently justified transformations, not blanket exceptions to v1 overlap/roundtrip checks. Confidence/BIO/control/UNK/unsupported/provenance/partial/overflow errors remain hard failures. Keep all planned witnesses, failures and zero-forward admission decisions in receipts. Any v2 criterion requires its own reviewed source/protocol/freeze; v1 remains unchanged and failed global proof cannot become a pass retrospectively.
